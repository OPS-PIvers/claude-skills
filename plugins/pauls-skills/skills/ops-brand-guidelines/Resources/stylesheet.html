Exit tickets take three minutes and show who's lost
Twelve Orono Middle School teachers tried them for five weeks last fall.

The teachers handed out three questions at the door every day. Of the 212 students in those classes, 84% answered every day.

Sorting the answers took about two minutes after class. Teachers started the next lesson with the question most students missed.

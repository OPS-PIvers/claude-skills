Dark (blue dark) is the default for slides. Content sits on the background. Columns are separated by space and blue light hairlines.

Content slides get claim titles that state the point. Title, section, agenda, and closing slides keep short labels.

One large number or title word per slide may be red (#d06f70, the tint that passes contrast on blue dark). Every statistic gets a source line.

Primary Logo can't sit directly on blue dark. Use the Secondary Logo, the Torch Icon at 1.5 to 2.5 inches, or the Primary Logo on a white plate.

Leave these out

  • A colored stripe on one side of any box
  • Accent lines under titles or before section labels
  • Uppercase letter-spaced section labels
  • Decorative icons, and icons in colored squares
  • Shadows and glows
  • Cards around lists or text, and cards inside cards
  • Status pills and badges
  • Zebra-striped tables and filled header rows
  • Em dashes, "not X but Y," and groups of three for rhythm
  • Delve, leverage, robust, seamless, empower, unlock
Lexend and Roboto only. Fallbacks: Calibri, then Arial.
